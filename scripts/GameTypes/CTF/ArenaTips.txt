&?swap <class> changes class: inf hvy jt sl med eng infil. Dead? It switches you when you respawn.
&?b list shows the ready-made builds for your class. ?b <name> buys one, ?b <name> info explains it.
&?bw <build or items> wipes your inventory first, then buys. One command, a clean loadout.
&?short prints your current inventory as a ?bw line. Paste it in a macro to rebuy exactly this kit.
&?ab (autobuy) rebuys your ?b / ?bw build every time you die or recall to the dropship.
&?ap (autopickup) tops you up from ammo piles you walk over, up to your build's amounts.
&?ao (autooccupy) puts you in your ride on respawn: hoverboard (eng/med), drop pack (jt), flight pack (inf/sl).
&?auto switches on autobuy, autodrop and autopickup at once (and autooccupy for jt / eng).
&?bwd <build> buys your build plus spare ammo, then ?d drops the spare for your team.
&?stim and ?energizer use those items. Put them in a macro and free up two keys.
&?as (autosummon) asks for a summon to your team's X every time you respawn.
&?stats shows everyone's accuracy. ?stats <alias> shows one player.
&?itemusage shows what players still had when they died. Carrying too much ammo? It'll tell you.
&?duel takes you to the dueling arena. ?p puts you in spec as ready for the next mix / OvD round.
